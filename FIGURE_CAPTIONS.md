### Slide 3: Virion Architecture
- **`figures/image4.png`**:
  - **Caption**: **Schematic Architecture of the Influenza Virion**. Shows an enveloped negative-sense RNA virus. Surface glycoproteins Hemagglutinin (HA) and Neuraminidase (NA), along with the M2 ion channel, are embedded in the host-derived viral lipid envelope. The interior matrix is lined by Matrix protein 1 (M1), enclosing eight segments of viral ribonucleoprotein complexes (vRNPs). Each vRNP consists of negative-sense genomic viral RNA (vRNA) encapsulated non-specifically by oligomeric nucleoprotein (NP) and associated at the partially complementary conserved ends by the trimeric RNA-dependent RNA polymerase complex (PB1, PB2, and PA).

### Slide 4: Viral Life Cycle Components
- **`figures/image5.png` - `figures/image15.tiff` / `.png`**:
  - **Caption**: **Steps of the Influenza Virus Life Cycle**. Vector components illustrating:
    1. Receptor-mediated endocytosis triggered by HA binding to cell-surface sialic acid receptors.
    2. Acidification of the endosome mediated by M2 ion channels leading to viral envelope fusion and uncoating.
    3. Import of vRNPs through nuclear pore complexes into the host nucleus.
    4. Nuclear viral mRNA transcription (cap-snatching mechanism) and genomic replication via complementary positive-sense cRNA intermediates.
    5. Nuclear export of newly synthesized vRNPs mediated by M1 and NEP/NS2.
    6. Assembly at the apical plasma membrane and budding of progeny virions facilitated by NA cleavage of sialic acid.

### Slide 8: Background Experimental Study
- **`figures/image24.png`**:
  - **Caption**: **Selective Downregulation of Influenza B Virus Promoter Activity by DDX3X**. Previous laboratory data generated by Mr. Soumik Dey demonstrating that overexpression of DDX3X selectively inhibits viral RNA synthesis in Influenza B virus (IBV) through interaction with the conserved 5' and 3' terminal promoter regions (untranslated regions, UTRs), while exerting no such inhibitory effect on Influenza A virus (IAV) promoter activity.

### Slide 11: DDX3X Expression & Affinity Purification
- **`figures/image26.png`**:
  - **Caption**: **SDS-PAGE Analysis of Recombinant DDX3X Expression**. Coomassie Brilliant Blue-stained 10% SDS-PAGE gel comparing whole-cell lysates of *E. coli* BL21(DE3) harboring the DDX3X expression construct. Lane UN: Uninduced bacterial lysate; Lane IN: Lysate post-induction with 0.5 mM IPTG, clearly revealing a prominent overexpressed band at the anticipated molecular weight of ~73 kDa corresponding to full-length His-DDX3X.

  - **Caption**: **Ni-NTA Affinity Chromatography Purification Profile**. SDS-PAGE analysis of elution fractions (E1 through E6) obtained from IMAC purification on a Ni-NTA agarose matrix. Demonstrates progressive elution of highly enriched, intact ~73 kDa recombinant DDX3X with minimal host protein background.

### Slide 18 & 19: Electrophoretic Mobility Shift Assays (EMSA)
- **`figures/image40.png`, `figures/image41.png`**:
  - **Caption**: **Principles of Electrophoretic Mobility Shift Assay (EMSA)**. Conceptual illustration showing complex formation between free radiolabeled RNA probe and increasing titrations of target protein, leading to retarded mobility on non-denaturing native polyacrylamide gels.
- **`figures/image42.png` (`.tif`)**:
  - **Caption**: **DDX3X Binding Titration to Influenza B PB1 Genomic Segment**. Native PAGE autoradiogram of binding reactions containing 5'-radiolabeled IBV PB1 segment incubated with increasing concentrations of purified recombinant DDX3X (0, 0.5, 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20 µM). Displays clear transition from unbound free RNA probe to high-molecular-weight DDX3X–PB1 RNA ribonucleoprotein complex.
- **`figures/image43.png` (`.tif`)**:
  - **Caption**: **DDX3X Binding Titration to Influenza B PB2 Genomic Segment**. Native PAGE autoradiogram of binding reactions containing 5'-radiolabeled IBV PB2 segment incubated with titrating concentrations of purified recombinant DDX3X (0 to 20 µM). Shifted bands demonstrate direct, dose-dependent interaction between host DDX3X helicase and the viral PB2 genomic segment.
